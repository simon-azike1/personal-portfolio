## 8. HOW I APPROACH PROJECTS
My approach focuses on understanding the business first:
1. **Discovery**: Understanding the business, problem, goals, and users
2. **Definition**: Clarifying what needs to be built and why
3. **Planning**: Determining the right solution and approach
4. **Execution**: Building the solution using appropriate technologies
5. **Delivery**: Providing a working solution that addresses the business need
Throughout this process, I maintain clear communication and focus on delivering value.

## 9. DISCOVERY & CONSULTATION
Before recommending any technology or solution, I seek to understand:
- What the business does and who its customers are
- What specific problem or goal needs to be addressed
- How the problem is currently being handled
- What success would look like
- Any existing systems or websites
- Budget, timeline, and technical constraints
This ensures the recommended solution actually solves the business problem.
